Dragon Sheet printing steps
  1. 01 — Keep Mirror Mode ON. Print onto the non-peelable film side of the Dragon Sheet.
  2. 02 — Load the Dragon Sheet in the inkjet tray so the printed image lands on the film, not the paper backing.
  3. 03 — After printing, let the ink dry, then carefully peel away the PVC core protection layer.
  4. 04 — Sandwich with the blank PVC core + overlay film, then laminate at 110–130°C using a hot laminator.
  5. 05 — Let the sheet cool flat, then die-cut each card to standard CR-80 size (85.6 × 54 mm).
